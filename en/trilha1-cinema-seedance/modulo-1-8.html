Seedance Cinema · Module 8/10

UGC Ads I · The Polished Commercial

A 30-second ad featuring a spokesperson you invented.

A 30-second ad featuring a spokesperson you invented. The commercial application: hero product shots, a speaking presenter, and a punchline, in a single generation. Swap out the brackets and this is a client deliverable.

A polished 30-second commercial with spoken dialogue for [PRODUCT NAME], a [one-line product description]. Bright, premium, modern advertising cinematography with crisp studio lighting.

AUDIO MIX: upbeat feel-good indie-pop track with claps and bright synths that ducks under every spoken line; dialogue warm and close-mic'd; a satisfying product sound effect on every hero shot.

SCENE ONE: a [presenter description, e.g., woman in her late twenties with dark curly hair wearing a mustard sweater] holds the [PRODUCT] toward the camera in a sunny modern kitchen and says with a genuine smile, perfectly lip-synced: "[Opening hook line, under 4 seconds]."

SCENE TWO: three fast, luxurious hero macro shots of the [PRODUCT]: [detail one], [detail two], [detail three], each landing with a tactile sound effect, with the soundtrack building underneath.

SCENE THREE: back to the presenter, now [using the product], she looks at the camera and delivers the closing line, lip-synced: "[Benefit line + call to action]." She winks as clean, bold text appears beside her, reading exactly "[PRODUCT NAME]", spelled perfectly, with a final musical sting.

Sound design: kitchen ambience, product foley on each macro shot, text pop as it appears, bright final hit.

Customize: the exact same presenter description in scenes one and three keeps your spokesperson consistent. This is the polished, produced version — the next two prompts are the ones that feel human-made.
