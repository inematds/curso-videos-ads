The Problem-Ritual-Result Ad

The Problem-Ritual-Result Ad Ideal for: supplements, coffee, productivity tools — anything people buy to solve an everyday problem.

Create a 30-second UGC creator ad with the Higgsfield MCP for RISE — fictional lion's mane focus gummies.

STEP 1 — PRODUCT (gpt_image_2, quality high, 3:4): "Clean e-commerce product photograph of an amber glass supplement jar with a black lid, kraft-paper label reading 'RISE · Lion's Mane Focus Gummies', a few translucent red berry gummies beside the jar on a light wood kitchen counter, bright daylight, photoreal, no watermark."

STEP 2 — CREATOR (soul_2, 3:4, quality 2k): "Unretouched casual iPhone front-camera style portrait of a man in his late 20s, short dark hair, light stubble, charcoal hoodie, sitting at a cluttered home desk with a laptop and coffee mug behind him, everyday apartment daylight, real skin texture, authentic amateur selfie framing, tired-but-friendly expression, no retouching."

STEP 3 — VIDEO (seedance_2_5, mode omni_reference, 9:16, 1080p, duration 30, generate_audio true, both job ids as image_references):

A 30-second vertical video, filmed in one take with the front-facing camera of an iPhone in selfie mode, with slight handheld movement. One continuous shot. The man from the reference is seated at his desk at home, holding the phone in one hand and speaking to the camera as if telling a friend a secret. The jar of gummies from the reference is the only product. 0-4s: 'So I used to hit a wall every single day at 2pm. Like, useless.' as he lifts the jar into the frame. 4-11s: He turns the label toward the lens: 'These are lion's mane focus gummies. I take two with my coffee, that's the whole routine.' 11-18s: He opens the jar, puts a gummy in his mouth, chews, and nods: 'They actually taste good, which... surprised me.' 18-25s: He gestures toward the laptop while still holding the jar: 'No jitters, no crash. I've cleared my whole list before lunch every day this week.' 25-30s: He leans in slightly and taps the lid: 'Just run it for one week. That's all I'm saying.' Realism: realistic skin texture with visible stubble, natural apartment lighting, authentic amateur framing, no beauty filter, no cinematic color grading, no on-screen text. AUDIO: his real voice, synchronized exactly with the quoted lines, relaxed and understated delivery, quiet apartment room tone, field recording quality; no music, no water drops, no bubbles, no synthesized sounds.

Customize: the moment of eating on camera is what sells a consumable product — never cut that part. For a drink, swap it for a sip and a small, honest exhale; for an app, swap it for taps on the phone screen.
