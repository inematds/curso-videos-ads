The Photo-Attached Review

The Photo-Attached Review Ideal for: YOUR actual product. Attach a photo or screenshot of it, fill in the brackets, and paste.

I attached a photo of my product. Create a 30-second UGC-style ad for it using the Higgsfield MCP. The product is [PRODUCT NAME] — [WHAT IT IS IN ONE LINE], and my customer is [WHO BUYS IT].

STEP 1 — RE-SHOOT MY PRODUCT: upload my attached image to Higgsfield, then use gpt_image_2 (high quality, 3:4) WITH that upload as a reference image: "Using the attached product photo as the exact reference, faithfully recreate this product as a clean e-commerce product photograph — identical shape, colors, label text, and logo — positioned on [a surface that suits the product], soft natural light, photorealistic, no watermark." Use the job ID of the RESULT from here on, never the raw upload — generated jobs go straight into video reference slots; raw uploads get blocked by the IP check.

STEP 2 — CREATOR (soul_2, 3:4, 2k quality): "Unretouched casual iPhone front-camera style portrait of [describe your ideal customer: age, appearance, clothing], in [the room where this product is used], natural light, real skin texture and no makeup, authentic amateur selfie framing, friendly relaxed expression, no beauty filter, no retouching."

STEP 3 — VIDEO (seedance_2_5, omni_reference mode, 9:16, 1080p, duration 30, generate_audio true, both job ids as image_references):

"Continuous, unedited 30-second vertical video, filmed with an iPhone front-facing camera in selfie mode, handheld, in a single take. The person from the reference is in [the same room], holding the phone at arm's length and talking to the camera like they're talking to a friend, with a slight natural handheld sway. The [PRODUCT NAME] from the reference is the only product present. 0-4s: start mid-thought while lifting the product into frame: '[ONE SENTENCE ABOUT THE PAIN THIS PRODUCT SOLVED — e.g., I was about to give up on ___ until this showed up.]' 4-11s: turn the label toward the lens: 'This is [PRODUCT NAME]. [ONE SENTENCE ABOUT WHAT IT IS AND WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT.]' 11-18s: use it on camera — [DEMO ACTION: apply / open / turn on / pour]: '[ONE SENTENCE REACTING HONESTLY TO USING IT.]' 18-25s: lean toward the lens with the proof: '[THE RESULT SENTENCE — what changed, stated as a fact to a friend, not as a claim.]' 25-30s: relaxed closing, product in focus: '[LOW-PRESSURE CLOSER — e.g., Just try it once. That's all it took for me.]' Realism: natural pore-level skin texture, natural light, authentic amateur framing, no beauty filter, no cinematic grade, no on-screen text. AUDIO: their real voice synced exactly to the quoted lines, natural conversational pacing, quiet dry room tone, field-recording quality; no music, no water droplets, no bubbles, no synthesized sounds."

Customize: the reshoot step isn't optional polish — it's what makes your real label hold up in the video AND lets the reference pass the platform's copyright check. A good phone photo of the product is enough; the model does the studio work.
