UGC Ads — Prompts · Module 4/7

The Nighttime Routine

The Cozy Gadget Demo

The Cozy Gadget Demo Ideal for: beauty devices, wellness gadgets, home tech — products with a visible “wow” moment.

Create a 30-second UGC creator ad with the Higgsfield MCP for a fictional LED red-light therapy face mask.

STEP 1 — PRODUCT (gpt_image_2, quality high, 3:4): "Clean e-commerce product photograph of a white silicone LED light-therapy face mask with rows of small red and amber LEDs glowing softly, resting on a marble vanity next to its white controller remote, soft evening lamp light, photoreal, no text on product, no watermark."

STEP 2 — CREATOR (soul_2, 3:4, quality 2k): "Unretouched casual iPhone front-camera style portrait of a Black woman in her early 30s, neat shoulder-length braids, glowing real skin with visible pores, oversized cream sweatshirt, sitting on her bed in a cozy warmly lit bedroom in the evening, string lights softly blurred behind her, authentic amateur selfie framing, warm genuine smile, minimal makeup, no retouching."

STEP 3 — VIDEO (seedance_2_5, mode omni_reference, 9:16, 1080p, duration 30, generate_audio true, both job ids as image_references — if a dark-mood preset is recommended instead of submitting, decline it and resubmit):

"Continuous, unedited 30-second vertical video, filmed with an iPhone front-facing camera in selfie mode, one single take with no cuts. The woman from the reference is sitting on her bed in her warmly lit bedroom, holding the phone at arm's length, with a warm, playful energy and a slight natural handheld sway. The LED mask from the reference, with its LEDs lit, is the only product. 0-4s: 'Everyone keeps asking what I changed about my skin... it's this thing.' as she lifts the mask into frame, LEDs already glowing red. 4-11s: turns the glowing mask toward the lens: 'Red light therapy mask. Ten minutes a night, that's it.' 11-18s: puts the glowing mask on her face, holds it for a moment, laughs: 'I know I look like a movie villain right now.' 18-25s: takes off the mask, leans toward the lens to show her cheek: 'But my dark spots are fading, and I'm literally doing this lying in bed.' 25-30s: hugs the mask against her chest, smiles: 'The laziest skincare routine I have. I'm obsessed.' Realism: natural pore-level skin texture, warm lamplight, authentic amateur framing, no beauty filter, no cinematic grade, no on-screen text. AUDIO: her real voice synced exactly with the quoted lines, warm and playful delivery, quiet dry bedroom room tone, field-recording quality; no music, no water droplets, no bubbles, no synthesized beeps."

Customize: the self-deprecating joke in the middle of the demo ("movie villain") does real work — it’s the moment that makes the whole ad feel human. Give every gadget ad a moment where the creator laughs at themselves.
